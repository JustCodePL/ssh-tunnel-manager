v1.0.17 STABLE 13 March 2026

Tunnel groups, pinned hosts, and refined UI

Groups can be connected and renamed, hosts can be pinned, and the interface is reorganized.

Groups and pinned hosts

Each group has a connect-all action and can be renamed, and the tunnel form has a group dropdown. Pinned hosts stay at the top of the list.

Interface

Labels are in English, colors are neutral and label colors are unified. The auto-connect toggle is restyled. Section headers, the file selector and the view order are reorganized. On Windows, the app falls back gracefully when sh is unavailable.

How to use it

  • Choose a group from the dropdown in the tunnel form.
  • Click connect all next to a group.
  • Click pin on a tunnel card.
