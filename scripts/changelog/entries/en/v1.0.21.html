v1.0.21 STABLE 27 May 2026

Better terminal clipboard and minimization

The terminal gets copy and paste shortcuts and can be minimized while the session keeps running.

Copy and paste

Ctrl/Cmd+C copies the selection, or sends SIGINT when nothing is selected. Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+C always copies and Ctrl/Cmd+V pastes. Right-click copies or pastes depending on the selection.

Minimize and restore

The terminal collapses to a floating pill in the bottom-right corner while the SSH session keeps running. Click the pill to bring it back, or use its close button to end the session. The terminal no longer covers the main window's titlebar, so you can drag, minimize, maximize and close the window while it's open.
