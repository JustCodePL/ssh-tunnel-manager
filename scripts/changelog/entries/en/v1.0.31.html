v1.0.31 STABLE 10 August 2026

Stable and Beta update channels

You can choose which update channel the app follows, and the choice is saved.

Update channels

Pick Stable or Beta in Settings. An update check started on one channel no longer carries over after you change channels.

Tray, macOS and the website

Tray menu rebuilds no longer produce duplicate or partly ordered items. macOS gets the loopback alias pool that portless forwards need. Settings has Website and GitHub shortcuts. The project now has a GitHub Pages download site with an FAQ, contact details and the full rollback archive.

How to use it

  • In Settings, under UPDATES, choose STABLE or BETA. Beta gets prereleases too.
  • Click check for updates to check right away.
